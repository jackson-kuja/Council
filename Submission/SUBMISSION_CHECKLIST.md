## Judging Criteria

| Criteria | Weight | How CoachBoard Addresses It |
|----------|--------|-----------------------------|
| **Audience Fit** | 30% | Built specifically for Simon's productivity/design-focused audience. AI coaching for entrepreneurs, creators, and systems thinkers. Personal context (values, goals) makes it deeply relevant. |
| **User Experience** | 25% | Clean, minimal SwiftUI design. Zero-setup onboarding — pick a coach, start talking. Voice-first interaction with beautiful animated orb. Dark theme, haptic feedback, Live Activities. |
| **Monetization Potential** | 20% | RevenueCat-powered subscriptions. Free tier with limited sessions, premium unlocks unlimited coaching, multi-coach sessions, Notion integration, and custom coach creation. |
| **Innovation** | 15% | Multi-coach sessions (up to 3 AI coaches with distinct voices in one conversation). MCP-powered Notion integration gives coaches access to your workspace. Per-user agent cloning for personalization. |
| **Technical Quality** | 10% | MVVM architecture, Firebase backend, ElevenLabs Conversational AI, Cloud Functions, Notion MCP proxy, Live Activities, deep linking, session continuity. |
